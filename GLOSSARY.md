# Glossary

- **Input event:** A keyboard, mouse-button, or scroll event counted by the local collector. The application does not store the typed character or cursor position.
- **Foreground interval:** A period during which a process is the foreground application. It is not proof of attention, work, or task completion.
- **Coverage:** The software-observable portion of a period in which the collector was expected to be active. Missing coverage is unknown, not zero activity.
- **Personal reference:** A comparison with the same user's prior observations under the project's stated eligibility rules. It is not a population norm or a quality score.
- **Activity density:** A count divided by a stated time denominator. It does not mean efficiency or output.
- **AI replay:** An optional interpretation of allowlisted aggregate facts by a user-selected provider. It is not a diagnosis.
